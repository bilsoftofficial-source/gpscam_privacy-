# Privacy Policy for GPS Cam — by Bilsoft

**Last Updated: 29 September 2026**

Bilsoft ("we", "us", or "our") operates the **GPS Cam — by Bilsoft** mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### a) Location Data
Our App collects your device's GPS location (latitude, longitude, altitude, and accuracy) to embed location information as a watermark on photos you capture. This location data is:
- Stored **locally on your device** only
- **Never transmitted** to our servers or any third party
- Used exclusively for the watermark feature

### b) Camera Access
Our App requires camera permission to capture photos. All photos are:
- Stored **locally on your device** in the "Pictures/GpsCam" folder
- **Never uploaded** to any server

### c) Reverse Geocoding
To display your address on the watermark, the App uses Android's built-in **Geocoder service**. This sends your coordinates to Google's geocoding servers for address lookup. Google's privacy policy applies: https://policies.google.com/privacy

### d) Device Sensor Data
The App accesses your device's **rotation vector sensor** (compass) to display direction (N, NE, E, etc.) on the watermark. This data is used locally only.

### e) App Update & Analytics Data
Our App uses **Google Firebase Remote Config** to check for available updates and to manage app configuration remotely. Firebase may collect the following non-personal information:
- App version and device model
- Operating system version
- Country/region (approximate, derived from IP address)
- Anonymous usage statistics

This information is used solely for delivering app updates and improving app performance. Firebase's privacy policy applies: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

---

## 2. Data Storage

All photos and metadata created by the App are stored **only on your device**. We do not have access to your photos, location, or any personal data.

The App stores the following preferences locally using Android's SharedPreferences:
- Watermark display settings (what to show/hide on photos)
- This data never leaves your device

---

## 3. Data Sharing

We **do not** share, sell, rent, or trade your personal information with any third parties. The only external services contacted are:
- **Google Play Services** (for location and geocoding)
- **Google's Geocoder** (for address lookup)
- **Google Firebase Remote Config** (for app updates and configuration)
- **Indus App Store** (for app distribution and updates)

These services operate under their respective privacy policies:
- Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
- Indus App Store: https://www.indusappstore.com/privacy
